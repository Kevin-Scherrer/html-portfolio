The Best Movies According to Kevin

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Ready Player One

I love this movie because it has so many videogame references.
It brings back many childhood memories.

Dune

This movie is bit on the slower side, but I absolutely love the Sci-Fi!
The storyline is fantastic!

Rush

Rush is an exhilarating movie! As a fan of Formula One, it captures
the rivalry of James Hunt and Niki Lauda. A must see for race fans!
